Pick a cloud

Why the cloud

Is the cloud right for your business?

For most financial firms it now is. Here is what changes when your platforms run on a hyperscale cloud instead of a server room.

  • Flexibility

    Pick from hundreds of managed services and shape them around your products, instead of shaping your products around the hardware you own.

  • Reliability

    Several availability zones and automatic failover keep client-facing platforms running through hardware faults and traffic spikes.

  • Scale and performance

    Capacity grows for market-open rushes, NFO launches and month-end runs, then shrinks again when the rush is over.

  • Pay for what you use

    Swap up-front hardware spend for usage-based pricing, with reserved capacity and savings plans that keep the bill predictable.

  • Security and compliance

    Encryption, identity controls and audit trails come built in, backed by certifications that support your own regulatory obligations.

  • India regions, global reach

    Data centres in India keep data close to your clients, and in the country when the rules require it, with worldwide regions for everything else.

Migration

Every workload gets its own path

We handle everything from a straight lift-and-shift to a complex hybrid set-up. These are the moves we make most often.

  • Data-centre exit

    Retire ageing on-premises hardware and hand the facility, refresh cycles and power bills to the cloud provider.

  • Application modernisation

    Re-platform or refactor core applications to use serverless functions, microservices and managed AI services.

    Stand up a recovery site in another region with automated failover, without buying a second data centre.

Questions

What clients ask us first

Which cloud should we choose?

It depends on what you already run, the skills in your team and the services you need. Microsoft-heavy estates often suit Azure, analytics-led work often suits Google Cloud, and AWS has the widest catalogue. We compare them for your workloads, costs included, before you commit.

Can you migrate us without downtime?

Most moves need a short, planned window for the final switch-over. Continuous replication keeps it down to minutes, we schedule it outside business hours, and there is always a tested rollback plan.

Will our data stay in India?

It can. AWS, Azure and Google Cloud all have regions in India. We deploy there and set up backups and replicas so data stays in the country whenever your obligations require it.

Do you support hybrid and multi-cloud set-ups?

Yes. We connect on-premises systems through VPNs or private links such as AWS Direct Connect, Azure ExpressRoute and Google Cloud Interconnect, and keep tooling consistent across clouds with Terraform and Kubernetes.

What happens after go-live?

You choose. We can run the environment for you with managed services and regular cost reviews, or hand it over to your team with documentation, runbooks and training.
