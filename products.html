What is Finexa?

Finexa is a comprehensive mutual fund distribution platform designed to help Mutual Fund Distributors / Advisors manage their business from a single hub. With features such as client onboarding, transactions, reporting, and portfolio tracking, Finexa brings together the tools needed to run and grow your practice.

Who is Finexa designed for?

Finexa is built for Mutual Fund Distributors, wealth advisors, banks, broking firms, and financial institutions looking to have a comprehensive one stop platform for mutual fund distribution and tracking, simplify their operations and enhance the investor experience.

Which transaction platforms does Finexa support?

Finexa integrates with all the Mutual Fund Transaction platform — NSE Invest, BSE Star MF, and Mutual fund Utility (MFU), allowing advisors to execute mutual fund transactions through their preferred platform.

Can clients complete KYC and onboarding through Finexa?

Yes. Finexa supports digital client onboarding, including PAN verification, MF Transaction Account opening and can be done through both the Finexa website and Finexa Nova App (Do-it-yourself Investor Onboarding) to help reduce manual paperwork.

Does Finexa support SIP Top-Up and NFO investments?

Yes, but currently only through NSE. MFDs can offer SIP Top-Up and New Fund Offers (NFOs) directly through Finexa. An update for including it with BSE Star MF platform is currently in development.

Can I track all my client's investments in one place?

Yes. Finexa provides a consolidated portfolio view, helping MFDs monitor investments, track performance, and access client information from a single dashboard. You can also generate and share detailed Transaction Report, Portfolio Valuation Reports, Portfolio Gain /Loss Report across all Mutual fund Investments of the client

Can Finexa help with goal-based investing?

Yes, MFDs can create investment goals for clients and track their progress over time, making financial planning more engaging and meaningful. Fin exa has a very comprehensive goal planning, investing and Goal tracking user journey. It also enables you to earmark any of the existing investments of the investor to a particular goal

Does Finexa support non-mutual fund holdings?

MFDs can maintain records of assets such as fixed deposits, equities, insurance, and other investments to build a broader financial picture for their clients, however in terms of transactions, only MF transactions are available.

Can reports be shared directly with clients?

Yes. Reports can be generated and downloaded or shared digitally through email, helping MFDs communicate portfolio updates quickly and professionally.

What reports are available?

Finexa offers a wide range of client and business reports. Client Reports include Portfolio Summary Report, Transaction Statements, Capital Gain /Loss reports, Performance reports etc. Business Reports include Business MIS, AUM Reconciliation Report, Brokerage MIS Reports, SIP Valuation

Can I manage multiple ARNs from a single Finexa account?

Yes. Finexa allows you to manage multiple ARNs at no additional cost, making it ideal for firms or MFDs operating under more than one ARN.

Is my client data secure?

Yes, protecting your data is a priority. Finexa uses advanced encryption (AES-GCM), which ensures both strong protection and data integrity in the background.

What is Finny?

Finny is Finexa’s built-in AI assistant designed to help you quickly find information about the platform. Whether you have questions about features, reports, or workflows, Finny is there to assist you.

Can I access Finexa from anywhere?

Yes. Finexa is a web-based platform, allowing you to securely access your business from anywhere with an internet connection. Investors can also stay connected through the Finexa Nova mobile app and MFDs can stay connected through the Finexa Mobile app.

Will my clients receive updates about their investments?

Yes. Through Finexa Nova and various reporting options, clients can stay informed about their portfolios while MFDs can easily share reports and updates whenever required.

Does Finexa have a mobile app for investors?

Yes. Finexa Nova enables investors to view their portfolios, access reports, complete transactions, track goals, and stay connected through a convenient mobile experience. The app is available on Android as well as iOS platforms.

Can I customise the investor app with my own branding?

Yes. Finexa offers white-label branding options at a separate cost, allowing MFDs and institutions to present the platform under their own identity.

How can Finexa help me engage with clients?

Finexa includes ready-to-use white-labeled marketing creatives that MFDs can share with clients to stay connected and maintain regular communication.

Can I control what my investors can do on the app?

Finexa includes ready-to-use white-labelled marketing creatives that MFDs can share with clients to stay connected and maintain regular communication.

Can I research and compare mutual funds within Finexa?

Yes. Finexa provides detailed fund information along with fund comparison tools to help MFDs evaluate schemes and support investment decisions.

Can Finexa recommend schemes based on client risk profiles?

Yes. MFDs can build their own recommended product lists, allowing Finexa to accordingly suggest schemes based on each client’s risk profile.

How quickly can I get support if I face an issue?

Our support team is committed to providing prompt assistance. Most queries receive a quick response, with most issues resolved within 48 hours.

Is training provided after I subscribe?

Yes. We provide onboarding assistance, assistance for NSE/BSE/MFU registration, platform demonstrations, and ongoing support to help MFDs get the most from Finexa.

Can I try Finexa before subscribing?

Yes. Contact our team to schedule a demo or explore the platform through a trial, so you can see how Finexa fits your business before making a decision.

Is Finexa fully white-labeled?

Yes. Finexa is a fully white-labelled platform where you can update your logo and information to reflect in real time on the main page, reports, creatives and the Finexa Nova App loading screen.

How often is my portfolio data updated?

Finexa updates portfolio,, transaction and MF NAV data every day, ensuring you always have access to the latest available information for your clients and business.

Does Finexa charge any commission on my transactions?

No. Finexa does not deduct any commission or levy additional transaction charges on your mutual fund business. Your earnings remain yours.

What is Quick Access?

Quick Access is a navigation tool that helps you reach frequently used pages and features in just a few clicks, reducing the time spent searching through menus.

Can I switch to Finexa if I am already using another platform?

Yes, many MFDs have transitioned to Finexa from other platforms. Provided you are operating under your own ARN, our team can guide you through the onboarding process and help you understand the options available based on your existing setup.

I'm a new ARN. Is Finexa suitable for me?

Yes. Finexa is designed to support MFDs from the very beginning of their journey, providing the tools needed to onboard clients, execute transactions, generate reports, and manage a growing practice.

What is Finexa GenNxt?

It is a wealth management stack: a fully integrated digital platform designed to streamline operations for family offices and wealth management firms, from the back office through to end clients.

Who is the platform built for?

Family offices and wealth management firms. The B2B2C model means the firm runs its own operations on the platform and serves its end clients through it.

What does B2B2C mean here?

Finlabs provides the platform to your firm, and your firm serves its clients on it. Service delivery runs from the back office to the end client without a break in the chain.

How is the platform structured?

In three layers: the channels and presentation layer (front office, back office and external interfaces), the key app components in the middle, and the data and integration layer underneath.

Which app components are included?

User management, data aggregation and management, portfolio insights, model portfolios and recommendations, reporting and dashboards, and revenue tracking and reconciliation.

Is there a mobile app?

The platform is a responsive web app, with a mobile app option. Clients can reach real-time portfolio reports and insights on both.

Can clients see their own portfolios?

Yes. The client dashboard gives them access to real-time portfolio reports and insights through the web and mobile apps.

How does onboarding work?

Onboarding is digital and designed to be smooth and efficient, and sits alongside investment planning and rebalancing as part of the advisory services on the platform.

What does multi-asset data aggregation give me?

Consolidated portfolio data across assets, which is what the portfolio insights and reporting are built on — so the numbers you act on are complete.

What reporting is available?

Real-time data visualisation and performance tracking, with reporting and dashboards for the roles that need them — including a dedicated RM dashboard.

What does the RM dashboard show?

Actionable insights for relationship managers: families, clients, RMs and prospects, with current value, AUM held, AUM held away and gain or loss per client.

How are transactions placed?

Through BSE StAR and MFU integration, so orders are placed from inside the platform rather than in a separate system.

How is KYC handled?

KYC APIs are part of the integration layer, along with security data providers, so checks and security master data come in over APIs.

Is revenue tracked on the platform?

Yes. Revenue tracking and reconciliation is one of the key app components, with comprehensive tracking and reconciliation tools.

How secure is it?

Security is enterprise-grade, aimed at top-tier data protection and compliance, with audit and compliance support so industry standards are met with ease.

Can it work with the systems we already run?

The data and integration layer is where external interfaces, security data providers and KYC APIs connect, and it is the layer to talk to us about for your own systems.

How does the platform help the business grow?

Proactive insights support better investment decisions, and firms see increased client retention, cross-selling and referrals as the client experience improves.

How can Finaware help my organization manage IAPs?

Finaware simplifies the entire lifecycle of IAP management — from scheduling sessions, uploading attendance sheets and tracking expenses to monitoring trainer performance and ensuring compliance. It gives your team an end-to-end dashboard to stay organized and audit-ready.

How does Finaware manage user access and permissions across different roles?

Finaware uses a role-based access system, so every user sees and performs only what is relevant to their role. Admins get full control to manage users, programs, compliance data and reports. Training agencies can plan sessions, assign trainers and monitor execution progress. Trainers handle on-ground activities such as attendance marking, photo uploads and session closure.

Is the platform safe and secure?

Definitely. Finaware uses encrypted storage and industry-standard security measures to ensure all your IAP data remains confidential and protected: 256-bit SSL encryption, a customised server port, servers hosted in a private zone, secure authentication with Keycloak, secure data storage, data encryption, role-based access control and continuous monitoring.

Can Finaware be customized as per our organization's workflow?

Yes. Finaware offers customizable modules, report formats, access levels and fields to align with your internal processes.

Is Finaware compliant with SEBI guidelines for IAPs?

Yes. Finaware is built with SEBI IAP requirements embedded into the workflow. Mandatory fields, approvals, document uploads and audit-ready reports ensure compliance at every stage of the program lifecycle.

Can participants register themselves for IAP events?

Yes. Participants can self-register using a registration link or QR code, making the process simple and scalable. Admins can also add participants manually or by bulk upload for closed events.

How easy is it for AMC teams to start using Finaware?

Finaware is intuitive, user-friendly and quick to onboard. Training and walkthrough support are provided to ensure a smooth transition.

What makes Finaware different from traditional IAP tracking methods?

Can we get a demo of Finaware?

Does Finaware support offline and online IAPs?

Does Finaware support regional language IAPs?

Yes. Finaware supports multilingual IAPs, allowing AMCs to plan, execute and track sessions across different languages to improve regional reach. Engagement reporting by language is an upcoming feature.

Does Finaware provide alerts or notifications?

Is Finaware suitable for both large and mid-sized AMCs?

What kind of support does Finaware offer post-implementation?
