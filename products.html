Why Finexa

Why financial professionals across the industry trust Finexa

Finexa is an all-in-one Mutual Fund Distribution platform designed to simplify operations, enhance client engagement and accelerate business growth. Available as a SaaS solution or an enterprise version, it is tailored to the needs of Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs) and financial advisors.

With its modular design, Finexa is cost-effective and ensures a faster go-to-market. The platform automates key workflows such as reporting and portfolio tracking, centralises client data and streamlines back-office processes — all while supporting your brand identity.

By integrating advanced tools for financial planning and client management, Finexa empowers MFDs to deliver efficient, transparent and high-quality investment experiences.

FAQs

Questions distributors ask us

What is Finexa?

Finexa is a comprehensive mutual fund distribution platform designed to help Mutual Fund Distributors and advisors manage their business from a single hub — client onboarding, transactions, reporting and portfolio tracking in one place.

Who is Finexa designed for?

Mutual Fund Distributors, wealth advisors, banks, broking firms and financial institutions looking for one platform for mutual fund distribution and tracking.

Which transaction platforms does Finexa support?

Finexa integrates with NSE Invest, BSE StAR MF and Mutual Fund Utility (MFU), so advisors can transact through their preferred platform.

Can clients complete KYC and onboarding through Finexa?

Yes. Finexa supports digital client onboarding, including PAN verification and MF transaction account opening, through both the Finexa website and the Finexa Nova app.

Can I manage multiple ARNs from a single account?

Yes. Finexa lets you manage multiple ARNs at no additional cost, which suits firms and MFDs operating under more than one ARN.

Is my client data secure?

Yes. Finexa uses advanced encryption (AES-GCM), which provides both strong protection and data integrity in the background.

What is Finny?

Finny is Finexa’s built-in AI assistant. It helps you find information about the platform, whether you have questions about features, reports or workflows.

Does Finexa charge any commission on my transactions?

No. Finexa does not deduct any commission or levy additional transaction charges on your mutual fund business. Your earnings remain yours.

Can I try Finexa before subscribing?

Yes. Contact the team to schedule a demo or explore the platform through a trial, so you can see how Finexa fits your business before deciding.
