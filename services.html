02

Application & Infra Audits

Find the weak points before anyone else does.

A thorough analysis and evaluation of your applications and infrastructure to identify vulnerabilities, optimize performance and enhance security — delivered as findings ranked by severity and a remediation plan sequenced by risk against effort.

What we do

  • Static and dynamic application security testing
  • Dependency, SBOM and open-source licence review
  • Infrastructure-as-code and CIS benchmark checks
  • Load and performance profiling under peak traffic

You walk away with

  • Findings scored by CVSS severity with evidence
  • A remediation roadmap your team can execute
  • Performance baselines and latency budgets
  • An executive summary for your board or regulator

03

Cybersecurity

Security your regulator and your clients can rely on.

Comprehensive threat assessment, advanced defense strategies and proactive risk mitigation to safeguard your digital assets. We map your real attack surface, harden it around zero-trust principles, and wire in detection your team can actually run.

What we do

  • Threat modelling and attack-surface mapping
  • Identity redesign: zero trust, IAM roles and MFA
  • Encryption at rest and in transit with managed keys
  • Penetration testing and incident-response drills

You walk away with

  • A risk register with owners and treatment plans
  • Controls mapped to ISO 27001 and SOC 2
  • SIEM detections and SOAR response playbooks
  • Evidence packs ready for audit

  2. Harden
