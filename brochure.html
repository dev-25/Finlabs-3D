How it began

From day one the focus has been the same: simplify finance through smart, holistic technology. In 2016 two founders left comfortable corporate careers to build something of their own, and a deep look at the BFSI space turned up gaps and inefficiencies that could not be ignored — complex legacy infrastructure, siloed data, rising regulatory cost and slow, expensive product development.

Ten years on, that is still the job: readymade product stacks where a firm needs to move quickly, and end-to-end development where it needs something of its own.
