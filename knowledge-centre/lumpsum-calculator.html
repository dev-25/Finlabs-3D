Behind the numbers

How the maths works

The formula

Total = P × (1 + r)t

P is the amount invested, r the yearly rate and t the number of years.

What it means

The whole amount works from day one, which is why the curve starts slowly and steepens later: the returns themselves start earning. It also means a fall soon after you invest hurts more than it would with a monthly plan.

The other way round

Investing out of what you earn each month? A SIP puts the same amount in every month, so the price you pay averages out over time.
