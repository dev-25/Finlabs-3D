FAQs

What AMCs ask us about Finaware

18 questions about managing IAPs. Type to find one.

How can Finaware help my organization manage IAPs?

Finaware simplifies the entire lifecycle of IAP management — from scheduling sessions, uploading attendance sheets and tracking expenses to monitoring trainer performance and ensuring compliance. It gives your team an end-to-end dashboard to stay organized and audit-ready.

How does Finaware manage user access and permissions across different roles?

Finaware uses a role-based access system, so every user sees and performs only what is relevant to their role. Admins get full control to manage users, programs, compliance data and reports. Training agencies can plan sessions, assign trainers and monitor execution progress. Trainers handle on-ground activities such as attendance marking, photo uploads and session closure.

Is the platform safe and secure?

Definitely. Finaware uses encrypted storage and industry-standard security measures to ensure all your IAP data remains confidential and protected: 256-bit SSL encryption, a customised server port, servers hosted in a private zone, secure authentication with Keycloak, secure data storage, data encryption, role-based access control and continuous monitoring.

Can Finaware be customized as per our organization's workflow?

Yes. Finaware offers customizable modules, report formats, access levels and fields to align with your internal processes.

Is Finaware compliant with SEBI guidelines for IAPs?

Yes. Finaware is built with SEBI IAP requirements embedded into the workflow. Mandatory fields, approvals, document uploads and audit-ready reports ensure compliance at every stage of the program lifecycle.

Can participants register themselves for IAP events?

Yes. Participants can self-register using a registration link or QR code, making the process simple and scalable. Admins can also add participants manually or by bulk upload for closed events.

How easy is it for AMC teams to start using Finaware?

Finaware is intuitive, user-friendly and quick to onboard. Training and walkthrough support are provided to ensure a smooth transition.

What makes Finaware different from traditional IAP tracking methods?

Unlike spreadsheets or manual processes, Finaware offers centralized control, real-time visibility and built-in compliance, making IAP management smarter and future-ready. AI-driven insights are an upcoming feature.

Can we get a demo of Finaware?

Yes. AMCs can request a personalized demo to see how Finaware fits into their IAP ecosystem and compliance needs — use the Request a demo button on this screen.

Does Finaware support offline and online IAPs?

Yes. Finaware supports both online and offline IAPs, with features such as venue management, map links, attendance tracking and format-specific handling.

Does Finaware support regional language IAPs?

Yes. Finaware supports multilingual IAPs, allowing AMCs to plan, execute and track sessions across different languages to improve regional reach. Engagement reporting by language is an upcoming feature.

Does Finaware provide alerts or notifications?

Yes. Finaware triggers system notifications for approvals, pending actions and upcoming events, so nothing is missed.

Is Finaware suitable for both large and mid-sized AMCs?

Yes. Finaware is scalable and adaptable, making it suitable for AMCs of all sizes, regardless of the number of IAPs conducted.

What kind of support does Finaware offer post-implementation?

Finaware offers ongoing support, training and platform enhancements, ensuring smooth operations and continuous improvement.

Who can create invoices in Finaware?

Invoices can be created by the training agency and trainers only, and only after submitting all mandatory documents upon event completion.

Does Finaware prevent billing for incomplete or unapproved sessions?

Yes. Only completed and approved sessions are eligible for invoicing.

Can invoices be linked to specific IAP sessions?

Yes. Every invoice in Finaware is mapped to one or more completed IAP sessions, ensuring complete traceability and justification for billing.

Can Finaware work with our existing trainers and partners?

Yes. Finaware connects with existing L&D teams, trainers and channel partners, so the programme runs through the ecosystem you already have.
