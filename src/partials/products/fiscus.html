FAQs

Questions people ask about Fiscus

12 questions about the app. Type to find one.

What is Fiscus?

Fiscus is a personal finance app — your financial companion. It gives you a consolidated view of your financial assets, bills and investments, so financial management becomes simple, efficient and convenient.

Where can I get the app?

Fiscus is available on the app stores — use the Get the app button on this screen for the Google Play listing.

What can I see in one place?

All your financial assets, bills and investments: total bank balance, outstanding bills, this month's expense and income, your holdings with current value and gain or loss, and your recent transactions.

How do bill reminders work?

Fiscus sends timely reminders for bills that are due and for bills already overdue, so you stay on top of your commitments without tracking them manually.

Which investments can I track?

Stocks, mutual funds and retirement schemes, and users also track bank deposits and other investments alongside them — all in one portfolio, in real time.

Does it track my expenses automatically?

Yes. Fiscus simplifies expense tracking, categorization and budgeting, and classifies transactions for you rather than asking you to categorise each one by hand.

Do I have to share my passwords?

No. Fiscus eliminates the need to share sensitive information such as passwords.

Is my financial data secure?

Fiscus prioritizes the security of your financial data, ensuring it remains safe and confidential.

What is Find an Advisor?

It connects you with experienced financial professionals in your area for personalized advice, straight from the app.

I am an MFD or financial planner — can I be listed?

Yes. By signing up for Fiscus you are featured in the Find an Advisor section at no cost, and leads come directly to your inbox.

Does being listed cost anything?

No. Featuring in Find an Advisor is at no cost.

What does Fiscus save me?

Time and effort. Its automation and advanced features do the legwork, which is what gives you peace of mind over your financial management.
