Why Learngenie

A white-labeled platform built around the learner

Learngenie is a versatile, white-labeled Learning Management Platform designed to provide personalized and customized learning journeys. It supports gamification, multiple stakeholders and quick go-to-market solutions, with plans to suit different needs.

It suits corporates looking to implement efficient and engaging learning for their employees, as well as educational institutions and training organizations enhancing their learning management.

Personalized journeys

Learning paths tailored to each learner's profile, preferences and performance.

Gamified engagement

Points, badges, leaderboards and challenges that keep learners coming back.

Multiple stakeholders

Admins, trainers, compliance and content managers and participants, each with their own rights.

Quick go-to-market

Designed for rapid deployment, so training can start without a long build.

Everything organizations ask us

What is Learngenie?

Learngenie is a versatile, white-labeled Learning Management Platform designed to provide personalized and customized learning journeys. It supports gamification, multiple stakeholders, quick go-to-market solutions, and offers various plans to cater to different needs.

Who can benefit from using Learngenie?

Learngenie is ideal for corporates looking to implement efficient and engaging learning solutions for their employees. It is also suitable for educational institutions and training organizations seeking to enhance their learning management capabilities.

What features does Learngenie offer?

Personalized and customized learning journeys, gamification to increase engagement, support for multiple stakeholders, quick go-to-market solutions, a mobile application for on-the-go learning, self-paced learning modules, and webinars and live sessions.

Can Learngenie be customized?

Yes. Learngenie can be fully customized to meet the specific needs of your organization. Corporates can fill out a form to receive a customized solution and package tailored to their requirements.

What is gamification, and how does Learngenie implement it?

Gamification is the application of game-design elements and principles in non-game contexts to enhance user engagement. Learngenie incorporates it by integrating points, badges, leaderboards and challenges to make learning more interactive and motivating.

How does Learngenie support personalized learning journeys?

Learngenie uses advanced algorithms to tailor learning paths based on individual learner profiles, preferences and performance, so each learner receives the content and assignments most relevant to their needs and goals.

Can multiple stakeholders use Learngenie?

Yes. Learngenie is designed to support multiple stakeholders, including administrators, instructors and learners. Each stakeholder can have specific roles and permissions to manage and participate in the learning process effectively.

How quickly can we go to market with Learngenie?

Learngenie is designed for rapid deployment, allowing organizations to implement and start using the platform quickly. The exact timeline may vary based on customization requirements and the complexity of your organization's needs.

Is there a mobile application for Learngenie?

Yes. Learngenie offers a mobile application that allows learners to access their courses and learning materials on the go. The app supports all the features available on the desktop version, ensuring a seamless learning experience.

What types of learning methods are supported?

Learngenie supports self-paced learning modules, live webinars and interactive sessions, so learners can choose the method that best suits their learning style and schedule.

Can exams and assessments be conducted on Learngenie?

Yes. Learngenie provides tools for creating and administering exams and assessments. Instructors can design various types, including multiple-choice questions, essays and practical tasks, to evaluate learner performance effectively.

What course material can we upload?

HTML, PDFs, videos, documents, presentations, image slides, Excel sheets, assignments, assessments, polls, surveys, SCORM packages and exams — the platform supports diverse content formats.

How can organizations track learner progress?

Learngenie offers robust analytics and reporting features to track learner progress, performance and engagement. Administrators and instructors can access detailed reports and dashboards to monitor individual and group progress.

How can we get support for Learngenie?

Learngenie offers comprehensive support to its users. You can contact the support team through the platform's email or by phone for any technical assistance or queries.

How can corporates get a customized solution?

Corporates can fill out the form available on our website to request a customized solution. Our team will get in touch to discuss your requirements and create a tailored package that fits your needs.
