What you earn

A share of the first year

Commission is worked out on the first year's subscription, net of taxes, and is paid once the client has paid us. Refer more in a financial year and the rate steps up — it applies to every deal closed in that year, not just the ones after you cross the line.

The rules, in short

So there are no surprises later

  • The firm has to be new to us. If they are already a client, or already in a live conversation with our team, the referral does not apply. We will tell you within two working days either way.
  • First registration wins, and it lasts 90 days. Register a firm and it is yours for 90 days. If two people register the same firm, the earlier one counts.
  • Commission follows money we have received. We pay on what the client actually pays, not on what was signed. If they pay in instalments, you are paid as the instalments land.
  • You invoice us. We pay against an invoice, by bank transfer. GST and TDS apply as the law requires; if you are not registered for GST, that is fine, say so.
  • Speak for yourself, not for us. Please do not quote prices, promise features or use the Finlabs name in advertising without asking us first.
  • Ask before you share someone's details. Only send us contact details you have permission to share — the form asks you to confirm it.

The full terms go into the referral agreement we send you. We can change the programme with notice, and anything already registered keeps the terms it was registered under.

Questions

The things people ask first

How many firms can I refer?

As many as you like. The rate steps up with the number that sign in a financial year, and the higher rate applies to every deal closed in that year.

What if they take a year to decide?

Your registration holds for 90 days. If the conversation is still alive at the end of it, tell us and we will extend it — we are not looking for a technicality.

Do I need a company or a GST number?

No. Individuals can refer too. You will need to raise a simple invoice, and tax is deducted as the law requires.

Do I have to sell anything?

No. The introduction is the job. We run the demo, answer the questions and handle the paperwork.

Will you tell me what is happening?

Yes — we will let you know when we make contact, when a demo happens and when it closes or goes quiet.

Can I refer my own employer?

Check your own workplace rules first. If your employer is fine with it, so are we.
