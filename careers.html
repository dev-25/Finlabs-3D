The role

We are seeking an exceptional Project Manager – Custom Fintech Solutions to own end-to-end delivery of bespoke financial technology products across our wealth management, bond market and investor platform verticals. This is not a coordination role — it is a delivery leadership position for someone who has worked at the confluence of financial domain expertise and modern software engineering.

You will lead a cross-functional team of 8+ — frontend engineers, backend engineers, Flutter developers, QA specialists and a business analyst — translating complex financial requirements into production-grade, scalable solutions, on time and within scope. You will report to the CTO / Chief Delivery Officer.

Project leadership & delivery

  • Own the full project lifecycle — requirements, sprint planning, UAT, deployment and post-go-live stabilisation
  • Deliver bespoke wealth management platforms, bond market solutions and investor-facing products
  • Manage parallel workstreams across multiple clients and product lines
  • Establish milestone governance, sprint cadence and release readiness checkpoints

Business requirements & documentation

  • Lead BRD and FRD/SRS authoring with business analysts, turning client intent into precise technical specifications
  • Run deep-dive discovery workshops with BFSI clients to extract, validate and prioritise requirements
  • Maintain living documentation that keeps every stakeholder on one source of truth

Team management

  • Lead and mentor frontend, backend and Flutter developers, QA engineers and a business analyst
  • Allocate resources across sprints and resolve blockers before they become escalations
  • Build a culture of accountability, continuous improvement and technical excellence
  • Run 1:1s, performance reviews and structured retrospectives

Stakeholder & client management

  • Be the interface between Finlabs leadership, engineering teams and BFSI clients
  • Manage expectations transparently — flag risks early and communicate trade-offs
  • Deliver executive-level status reporting, dashboards and escalation protocols

Process, quality & governance

  • Run sprint planning, stand-ups, reviews and retrospectives with precision
  • Build QA cycles, regression testing and UAT into timelines rather than bolting them on
  • Own JIRA boards end to end: epics, stories, velocity tracking and burndown analysis
  • Enforce change management and scope governance on bespoke builds

Domain expertise

  • Wealth management platforms, bond markets, mutual fund ecosystems and compliance frameworks (SEBI, RBI, AMFI)
  • Financial product lifecycles: portfolio management, fixed income, IAP mandates and digital distribution

Tools

  • JIRA (advanced) and Confluence
  • Monday.com, Asana or ClickUp; Notion
  • Microsoft Project or Smartsheet
  • Slack or Microsoft Teams; Miro and Figma

Engineering acumen

  • Modern stacks: React or Angular, Node.js, Python, Java/Spring Boot, Flutter, AWS/Azure/GCP
  • Reviewing architecture proposals and making informed delivery trade-offs
  • REST APIs, microservices, CI/CD and DevOps as they affect release governance
  • AI/ML in fintech products is a distinct advantage
  • Data privacy, DPDP Act compliance and BFSI regulatory constraints

Methodologies

  • PMP, PRINCE2, PMI-ACP, CSM or equivalent — at least one required
  • Hands-on Agile/Scrum; SAFe is a strong plus
  • Requirement elicitation, story pointing, UAT, release management and hypercare

What you bring

  • 10+ years overall, with at least 5 in BFSI or fintech delivering custom software
  • A track record of complex, multi-stakeholder fintech projects delivered on time, budget and scope
  • Managing cross-functional engineering teams of 6–10 with accountability for delivery
  • Strong BRD/SRS authoring — as comfortable in a client workshop as in backlog grooming
  • Exceptional communication: you can brief a CXO and a developer on the same day
  • High ownership — you act without waiting for direction
  • Fluency with delivery data: velocity charts, risk registers, utilisation dashboards

Bonus points

  • Bond market platforms, fixed income digital distribution or algo-driven portfolio tools
  • SEBI-regulated platforms, investor education mandates or AMFI-compliant products
  • LMS or EdTech platforms in a financial services context
  • AI-augmented product development; an MBA, CFA or FRM is an edge, not a requirement
